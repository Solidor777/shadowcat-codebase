# Combat: defeated combatants, initiative groups, effect origins and display, the session pause

Read this before touching `CombatantEngine.defeated` or `.group`, `COMBATANT_GROUP_DOC_TYPE`, `rebuild_order`'s slot logic, `roll_with_groups`, the client combat groups API, `EffectsList`, the `expiryNotice` setting or the tracker's paused state.

## Defeated

- `CombatantEngine.defeated` (`#[serde(default)]`, false) marks a combatant out of the fight. `transition`'s `settle_turn` auto-resolves a combatant when `is_event || defeated || (hidden && TurnControl::OwnerMayEnd)`. The `defeated` term is independent of `hidden` and independent of `turn_control`: a defeated combatant auto-resolves under every `TurnControl`, runs its boundary steps (`turn_start`/`turn_end` effect sweeps) like any auto-resolved entry, and its document, resources and effects stay alive so reviving is one write.
- Client: `CombatApi.setDefeated(combatantId, defeated)` writes `/engine/defeated`; `CombatantRow` shows the toggle (`data-testid` `combat-tracker:defeated-<id>`, `aria-pressed`), enabled for `can.edit` or `mayEditResource`.

## Initiative groups

- A group is a `combatant-group` document (`COMBATANT_GROUP_DOC_TYPE`, engine `CombatantGroupEngine { name, color }`) that is always a child of a `combat`. `validation::validate_containment` requires a parent and forbids embedding; the persistence chokepoint checks the parent is a `combat`. A member names its group through `CombatantEngine.group`; the group stores no member list and no initiative. A dangling `group` reference contributes nothing (like a dangling `token_id`).
- `CombatSnapshot.groups` carries them. `transition::rebuild_order` gives all members ONE sort slot: the slot's initiative and tiebreak are the members' highest, and a full tie falls back to the first-appearing member's position in the reconciled list. Every member therefore carries an identical sort key, which keeps them contiguous even when a member's own initiative drifted. An ungrouped combatant is its own slot, so combats without groups sort exactly as before.
- Rolling: a `CombatRoll` entry's id may name a combatant or a group. `combat::roll_target_combatants` is the ONE expansion (the combatant, every member of the group, or `None`); `authorize` and `build_ops` both read it, so the combatants checked are exactly the combatants written. A group entry stamps ONE result on every member and posts ONE chat message (`transition::roll_with_groups`), `Audience::GmOnly` when any member is hidden. Authorization is all-or-nothing: the caller must own EVERY member (`owns_combatant` with `CombatantAct::WritesEngine`), and naming a group and one of its members, or the group twice, is a duplicate. A group rolls against its first member's host for the formula's references.
- Client: `buildCombatantGroupDoc`, `CombatApi.groups`/`createGroup`/`setGroup`, the tracker model's `rowsFor(combatants, resolved, groups)` and `rollTargets` (collapses a group's members to ONE target, the group id, when some member has no initiative yet and the viewer is the GM or owns every member; otherwise the group is skipped), the add-combatants picker and the row's group stripe.

## Effect origins and display

- `EffectOrigin`/`ExpiryNotice` are exported from `@shadowcat/core` and `src/types`. The `expiryNotice` setting resolves through the same engine -> system -> world -> scene chain as every combat default: `ENGINE_COMBAT_DEFAULTS.expiryNotice` is `visible`, `SettingPath` `combat.expiryNotice` reports its provenance, and the shared provenance fixtures (`combat-provenance-cases.json`) carry cases for it that the Rust fixture tests also read. The settings editors (`CombatSettings`, `CombatSceneOverrides`) offer the select and the effective row.
- `@shadowcat/ui-kit`'s `listEffects(host, lookup, unnamed, anchor?)` is the client mirror of the server's `combat::effects::collect_effects` host walk (transfer and anchor rule included); change one and the other together. `effectOriginText` shows the origin label, else the origin's source document name, else nothing: an origin whose source is an embedded item shows no text unless it is labelled. `EffectsList` mounts in `CombatantRow` (compact) and in `ActorSheet`; `SheetHost` has no extension point for it.

## Session pause

A paused session freezes non-GM play. `resolveSessionPause(store)` and `sessionFreezes(store, role)` (`@shadowcat/core`, `scene-docs`) are the client predicates: the GM is never frozen, a spectator is. The server refuses a non-GM's frozen request with `RejectReason::Paused` (`ws::session_pause::blocks_msg`; the `Reject`, move-error and combat-error frames carry it) and the client surfaces it as `SessionPausedError`. `CombatTrackerPanel` derives `frozen` and passes it down: it shows a paused status line, busies the header and rows, disables the add fieldset, row drag handle and initiative inputs, and ignores reorder gestures. Selection, doors and emotes stay usable, so other panels do not blanket-disable their buttons. Scene tools read `ToolContext.movesFrozen` (see `shadowcat-codebase-scene-rendering`).
